Preguntas frecuentes

Preguntas frecuentes

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Every operation — cropping, resizing, converting, compressing and more — runs locally in your browser using the Canvas API and Web Workers. Tus imágenes nunca salen de tu dispositivo, which is why the tool works offline once loaded.

Is it really free?

Yes, the editor is completely free with no sign-up. It is supported by unobtrusive ads, which is why you may see a consent banner in some regions.

Which image formats are supported?

You can open PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF, GIF and BMP files, and export to PNG, JPG, WebP or AVIF. AVIF export depends on your browser supporting AVIF encoding.

How do I make an image smaller in file size?

Abre la pestaña Exportar, choose WebP or JPG, and lower the quality slider while watching the live file-size estimate. WebP and AVIF usually produce the smallest files at the same visual quality.

Does the editor remove EXIF and GPS metadata?

Yes. Because every export is re-encoded through the Canvas API, EXIF, GPS and other metadata are stripped automatically. También puedes ver los metadatos y descargar una copia limpia desde la pestaña Info.

Can I edit several images at once?

Yes. Add multiple images, switch between them in the thumbnail strip, and use “3. Descarga all (ZIP)” to export the batch as a single archive.
